Our Story

A Decade of Shared Horizons

From a rain-washed morning in Greenwich Village to quiet autumn weekends tracing the Hudson riverbank.

We met seven autumns ago over espresso and architectural sketchbooks in Lower Manhattan. What began as an afternoon debate on mid-century glass structures evolved into shared road trips up the Hudson Valley, countless home-cooked Sunday dinners, and a partnership built on curiosity, deep respect, and unhurried laughter.

Last November, along the misty bluffs overlooking the river in Rhinebeck, Julian asked Elena to marry him at dusk. As the river turned gold beneath the October light, she said yes. We cannot wait to gather the people who shaped our lives in this peaceful place we love most.

Arrival & Shuttles
